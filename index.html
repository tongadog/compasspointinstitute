What We Do

Three pillars of our work

Everything we pursue is oriented around a single question: how does Palau build institutions that earn public trust and endure?

01

Public Policy Discourse

Elevating informed, evidence-based dialogue on the issues shaping Palau's future, and creating space for rigorous debate outside of partisan pressure.

02

Transparent Governance

Promoting accountability, open data, and clear public process across government institutions at the national and state level.

03

Democratic Values

Strengthening the civic foundations — a free press, active civil society, and informed electorate — that safeguard Palau's sovereignty and self-determination.

Who We Are

Independent. Non-partisan. Rooted in Palau.

The Compass Point Institute operates independently of any political party or government body. We bring together researchers, practitioners, and community voices to examine the policy questions that matter most to Palau — and to the wider Pacific region it belongs to.
