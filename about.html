About Us

About The Compass Point Institute

A dynamic, independent think tank based in Palau.

Our Mission

Elevating discourse. Fostering transparency. Strengthening democracy.

The Compass Point Institute is a dynamic, independent think tank based in Palau. We are dedicated to elevating public policy discourse, fostering transparent governance, and strengthening democratic values across our nation and the broader region.

We believe that good policy starts with good information, and that Palau's institutions are strongest when they are open, accountable, and grounded in the trust of the people they serve.

What We Do

Our focus areas

01

Public Policy Discourse

We create space for rigorous, evidence-based debate on the issues shaping Palau's future — free from partisan pressure and open to a wide range of voices.

02

Transparent Governance

We advocate for accountability, open data, and clear public process across government institutions at every level, from the national government to local states.

03

Democratic Values

We work to strengthen the civic foundations — a free press, an active civil society, and an informed electorate — that safeguard Palau's sovereignty and self-determination.

Our Approach

Independent and non-partisan, by design

The Institute does not align with any political party or government body. Our independence allows us to ask difficult questions, follow the evidence, and offer analysis that policymakers, journalists, and citizens alike can rely on.

We are rooted in Palau, but our work engages with the broader Pacific region — recognizing that the challenges facing small island democracies are often shared across borders.

Want to learn more about our work?

Reach out to our team — we welcome partnerships, media inquiries, and conversations with anyone invested in Palau's future.
